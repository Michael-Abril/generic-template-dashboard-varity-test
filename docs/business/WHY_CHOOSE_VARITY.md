### How Varity is Different

**Varity doesn't just promise privacy. We PROVE it.**

Our **5-layer privacy architecture** makes it cryptographically impossible for us (or anyone else) to access your data:

#### Layer 1: Encryption at Rest (Lit Protocol)
**How it works**:
- Your wallet address is your encryption key
- All data encrypted **before** leaving your browser
- Only your wallet can decrypt
- Varity servers never see plaintext

**Traditional cloud**: "Trust us, your data is encrypted (with keys we control)"
**Varity**: "Your wallet, your key, your data - mathematically provable"

#### Layer 2: Distributed Storage (Filecoin/IPFS)
**How it works**:
- Files stored across thousands of Filecoin nodes
- No single point of failure or surveillance
- Content-addressed (tamper-proof)
- Redundant copies ensure availability

**Traditional cloud**: "Your files are on our servers (and we can read them)"
**Varity**: "Your files are everywhere and nowhere - encrypted chunks on decentralized network"

#### Layer 3: Data Availability (Celestia)
**How it works**:
- Proxy Data Availability (PDA) for encrypted data
- Zero-knowledge proofs verify data integrity
- No one can read the data, but everyone can verify it exists
- Blockchain guarantees data is available when you need it

**Traditional cloud**: "Trust us, we have backups"
**Varity**: "Cryptographic proof your data exists and is intact"

#### Layer 4: Decentralized Compute (Akash Network)
**How it works**:
- Your dashboard runs on decentralized Kubernetes
- No corporate surveillance (Google/Amazon don't control the servers)
- Geographic distribution for data sovereignty
- You choose where your compute runs

**Traditional cloud**: "Your app runs on our servers (we can monitor everything)"
**Varity**: "Your app runs on decentralized infrastructure (no corporate surveillance)"

#### Layer 5: Blockchain Settlement (Arbitrum + Ethereum)
**How it works**:
- All access requests recorded on-chain (immutable audit trail)
- Smart contracts enforce access control
- Wallet signatures for every action
- Transparent, verifiable, tamper-proof

**Traditional cloud**: "Trust our internal audit logs"
**Varity**: "Publicly verifiable blockchain records"
