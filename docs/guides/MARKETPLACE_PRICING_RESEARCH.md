## 6. Stripe (Payments)
**Developer:** Stripe
**Pricing Model:** Transaction-based (pay-as-you-go)

### Plans:
- **Standard**: No monthly fee
  - Pricing: 2.9% + $0.30 per successful transaction
  - International: Additional 1.5% cross-border fee + 1% currency conversion
  - In-person: 2.7% + $0.05
  - ACH: 0.8% (capped at $5)

- **Custom**: Contact sales for volume pricing
  - Pricing: As low as 2.2% + $0.30 for high-volume businesses
  - Features: Custom rates, dedicated support

**Additional Fees:**
- Disputes: $15 per dispute (new as of June 2025)
- Chargeback: $15 per chargeback

## Pricing Model Summary

### Fixed Tier Pricing (6 products):
1. QuickBooks
2. Shopify
3. Xero
4. FreshBooks
5. Mailchimp (contact-based)
6. Dropbox

### Per-User/Seat Pricing (11 products):
1. Salesforce
2. Slack
3. Monday.com
4. HubSpot (hybrid: per-seat + contacts)
5. Zendesk
6. Google Workspace
7. Microsoft 365
8. Asana
9. Trello
10. Intercom
11. Zoom (per-host)
12. DocuSign

### Usage-Based Pricing (2 products):
1. Stripe (transaction fees)
2. Twilio (per-message/minute)

### Key Observations:
- **Most Popular Model:** Per-user pricing (55% of products)
- **Annual Billing Discount:** Average 15-20% savings
- **Free Tiers:** 40% of products offer free plans
- **Enterprise Custom Pricing:** 60% of products
- **Minimum User Requirements:** Common for business plans (3-10 users typically)
